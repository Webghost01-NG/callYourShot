# Product brief

## Thirty-second explanation

Every round, a player predicts whether Bitcoin or Ethereum will finish higher
or lower than where it started. DreamDEX executes and publicly settles the real
trade. Call Your Shot then measures consistent decision quality, allowing
players to challenge friends and build a verifiable prediction record.

## Primary user

The initial user is a competitive retail crypto trader who understands basic
UP and DOWN predictions but wants recognition, fair competition, and evidence
of skill beyond raw profit.

## Problem

Conventional trading leaderboards over-reward large accounts, concentrated
risk, and isolated lucky outcomes. Screenshots and self-reported predictions
are not trustworthy records.

## Value proposition

Call Your Shot connects every qualifying result to a real DreamDEX fill and a
finalized Event Contract outcome. It ranks players using a published,
probability-aware method rather than total dollar profit.

## Minimum winning product

- One supported live BTC Event Contract cadence
- Clear UP or DOWN selection and bounded-risk review
- User-authorized DreamDEX order and verified fill
- Previously settled prediction with oracle evidence
- Transparent provisional skill score
- Small skill leaderboard
- Head-to-head challenge link without fund custody
- Shareable result receipt linked to verifiable evidence

## Non-goals for the hackathon MVP

- Custom market creation
- Custodial wallets or tournament escrow
- Copy trading
- Tokens, NFTs, or governance
- An agent marketplace
- An LLM-generated trading signal
- Claims of guaranteed performance or financial advice

## Product principles

1. Human competition is the product; AI is optional.
2. A submitted transaction is not presented as a filled prediction.
3. Only verified fills and finalized outcomes affect a score.
4. Voids and insufficient samples are shown honestly.
5. Blockchain terminology stays behind a simple consumer experience.
6. Maximum loss is visible before authorization.
