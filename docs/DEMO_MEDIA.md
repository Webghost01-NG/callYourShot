## Recording provenance

Recorded from the production application on 2026-09-08. Frames were sampled
from a real browser at two frames per second, assembled at 24 fps, and narrated
using synthetic speech. There are cuts between sections and a re-recorded ticket
section selecting genuine available liquidity. No UI data or outcomes were staged.

The sequence shows the homepage, live markets, order ticket, browser-wallet
chooser, completed two-wallet challenge, rematch navigation, and leaderboard.
No new wallet approval or order was signed during this recording. Fill and result
evidence belongs to the already executed challenge documented in
[growth-loop validation](GROWTH_LOOP_VALIDATION.md).
